The link gallery

A professional Streamlit console for turning EMK artwork links into a clear, searchable, measurable gallery workflow.

From scattered links to a curated collection with visible business priorities.
EMK LINKS / ART GALLERY CONSOLE01
The business challenge

Curated work deserves a reliable path to discovery.

Problem

Links are hard to manage

Artwork, artist, category, image, and destination data can become fragmented across messages, documents, and social channels.

Risk

Audience attention leaks

Broken or unreviewed destinations interrupt the visitor journey and weaken campaign confidence.

Opportunity

Make the collection actionable

One shared console connects curation, link health, catalog intelligence, and the next marketing move.

BUSINESS CONTEXT02
The solution

One console for the collection and the decision.

01 / Browse

Visual catalog

Artwork cards make the collection easy to scan by title, artist, category, and status.

02 / Curate

Protected controls

Curators can add, import, export, review, and restore catalog records.

03 / Measure

Business intelligence

Catalog health is visible through live coverage, review queue, link checks, and category mix.

04 / Act

Operational response

Every insight ends with a recommended action for curation, promotion, or repair.

EXECUTIVE VIEW05
Recommended operating rhythm

Use the console as a lightweight growth system.

Weekly

Curate the queue

Review draft and review items, confirm destinations, and select the strongest works for the next feature.

Before campaigns

Check the journey

Run link checks, repair failed destinations, and confirm that promoted works reach the intended page.

Monthly

Read the mix

Use categories, artists, status, and year span to shape programming and audience outreach.

Recommended next move: complete the review queue, validate every public destination, then publish a focused EMK collection.
